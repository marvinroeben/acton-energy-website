Company

Owners by conviction.

Acton Energy is a German independent power producer for grid-scale battery storage, based near Munich. We were founded to build storage infrastructure and to operate it for the long term.

Mission

A power system that is secure, affordable and renewable.

We believe those three qualities belong together. Wind and solar already deliver the energy. What they lack is reliability by the hour.

Storage closes that gap. Batteries absorb surplus when generation is strong and give it back when demand peaks. They turn variable production into dependable supply.

Acton builds and operates that flexibility infrastructure. We act with an owner's mentality and a long horizon: we develop, we build, we keep and we optimize what we own.
