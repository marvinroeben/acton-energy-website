Partners

Three ways to build with us.

We work with landowners, municipalities and project developers across Germany. Every partnership follows the same principle: clear terms, direct contact, and a counterpart who stays.

Landowners

Your land, a steady income, one counterpart.

We lease land for battery storage under long-term, index-linked agreements. Acton carries all development, construction and operating costs. You carry none.

At the end of the term we remove the plant completely. In our lease agreements we commit contractually to full decommissioning.

And because we do not sell our projects on, the counterpart you sign with is the counterpart who operates the site. Your contact stays the same.

What makes a site suitable

  • Around 2 hectares or more
  • Near a substation or a high-voltage line
  • Good access for construction and maintenance
  • Outside protected areas

Municipalities

Grid stability for the region, revenue for the community.

A battery storage plant is quiet, compact and unmanned, and it contributes to the place that hosts it.

Predictable trade tax

A storage plant generates steady trade tax revenue for the host municipality across its operating life.

Community participation

Financial participation for the host municipality, following the model established by the EEG, is possible. We discuss the framework openly.

A stronger regional grid

Storage supports the local network and helps connect more wind and solar in the region reliably.

Early, open communication

We engage early in the permitting process, keep councils informed and stand for dialogue with residents.

Talk to us before the formal process starts. We answer questions from councils and administrations directly.

Developers

A long-term partner for ready-to-build projects.

We acquire ready-to-build battery storage projects and enter co-development partnerships. Because we hold what we buy, we look for projects we want to operate for decades, and we review them accordingly.

Criterion 01

From 2 MW

We review projects from 2 MW upwards, as single sites or as portfolios.

Criterion 02

Located in Germany

Our focus is the German grid. That is where we develop, build and operate.

Criterion 03

Advanced grid connection

The grid connection process should be well underway. We assess its status early in the review.

Our process is reliable: a clear checklist, a fast review and a direct answer. No broker chains, no resale.
