Beyond the map

The pipeline behind the sites.

The eight projects above are the visible part of a 2.4 GW development pipeline. It combines four sourcing channels: opportunistic acquisitions, proprietary large-scale development, decentralized storage deployment and industrial partnerships.

Exclusive framework agreements with established German developers secure a predictable flow of ready-to-build projects on top of our own development work. We announce projects from these channels as they reach substance.

How we source and build

Land, steel, cells and grid connection: storage is infrastructure, and infrastructure is built site by site.
